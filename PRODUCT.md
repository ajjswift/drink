## Product Purpose

drink. is a mobile alcoholic drinks van based in Dorset, providing cocktail and mocktail service for events. It should help prospective event organisers understand its offering for their type of event and choose the service for it. Success is an organiser finding the relevant event information and progressing to a booking or enquiry; the exact conversion flow is undecided.

## Positioning

Each cocktail recipe is designed so its alcohol can be removed to create a mocktail that tastes almost the same. This makes the menu appropriate for alcoholic and non-alcoholic occasions without treating mocktails as a separate, lesser offer.
